The Healthcare Dilemma

Why Traditional Medical Records Fail in Emergencies

Current electronic medical records are designed for outpatient billing and desktop computers—not for an ambulance paramedic at 2 AM on a highway.

The Emergency Status Quo

  • Unconscious Victims Cannot Speak: Paramedics cannot know blood groups or contraindicated drugs (e.g. fatal Penicillin or Aspirin reactions).
  • Phone is Passcode Locked: Bystanders and ambulance crew cannot access emergency contacts stored inside the phone.
  • Fragmented Hospital Silos: Reports in Hospital A cannot be viewed by Emergency Room B in another city or state.
  • Family Disconnected: Next-of-kin often discover an accident hours later when police finally trace physical IDs.

The MedPass Breakthrough

  • Lock-Screen & Physical Card Access: Any phone camera scans the QR code directly from the lock screen wallpaper or physical credit-card wallet pass.
  • Two-Tier Privacy Protection: Tier 1 shows essential life-saving vitals in 3 seconds. Tier 2 requires Doctor Medical Council (NMC) verification.
  • Automated Kin Alert: As soon as the pass is scanned in an ER, next-of-kin receives an instant SMS with hospital location.
  • ABDM / ABHA Architecture: Seamlessly interoperable with India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission and EHR standard protocols.

Patient-Controlled Emergency Health Passport

Full-Stack Tech Architecture

  • • Backend: Python, FastAPI (High-performance Async API)
  • • Database: PostgreSQL / SQLite with indexed secure tokens
  • • Security: Tokenized QR payloads, NMC Doctor verification

Implementation Strategy

Does not require specialized hardware for initial deployment. Operates via standard phone cameras and printed QR cards. Risk mitigation relies on role-based authentication, minimal public data exposure, and instant SMS audit notifications.
